About Me

I’m currently a PhD student in Atmospheric Physics at ETH Zurich. I’m fortunate to be supervised by Prof. Ulrike Lohmann, Dr. Jan Henneberger, and Dr. Fabiola Ramelli. Previously, I received my M.Sc. in Meteorology from the University of Oslo (UiO), where I worked with Prof. Trude Storelvmo, Dr. Robert O. David, Prof. Alexander Binder, and Prof. Morten Hjorth-Jensen.
